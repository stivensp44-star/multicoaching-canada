A simple, transparent approach

This page explains, in general terms, how Multicoaching Canada handles information shared by people who contact us through this website.

Information you share

If you use the contact form, email link or telephone, you may choose to share your name, contact details, the service you are interested in and your message. We use this information to respond to your request and continue the conversation if you wish.

Technical information

Visiting a website may produce limited technical information, such as browser details or information needed for hosting security and operation. This site does not use advertising profiling or default analytics tracking.

Retention and sharing

We retain communications for a reasonable period to respond, provide relevant follow-up or meet applicable requirements. We do not sell your information. Information is not shared with a third party for a different purpose without a valid reason or authorization where required.

Contact form

The form on the home page is a request interface. Until a delivery provider is configured, submission is not activated. Direct phone and email links remain available.

Your questions and rights

You may ask for information about data concerning you, request a correction or ask us to stop unnecessary follow-up, subject to applicable requirements. Contact multicoachingmtl@gmail.com or 438-922-4181.
